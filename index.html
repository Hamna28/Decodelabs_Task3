Craftsmanship & Sustainability

Clean Burning, Thoughtfully Made

At Flora, every candle is hand-poured in small batches using 100% natural soy wax, lead-free cotton wicks, and pure botanical extracts. We believe luxury should never come at the expense of your health or the planet.

Our soy wax is derived from American farm-grown soybeans, burning up to 50% cleaner and longer than paraffin wax.

We blend fine fragrance oils with natural essential oils, ensuring zero toxic fumes or synthetic additives.
